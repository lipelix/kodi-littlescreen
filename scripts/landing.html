Little Screen

The YouTube Kids catalogue on Kodi: shows, music and learning, picked for each child's age. Big pictures for toddlers, a list with search for older children.

Unofficial, not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Version $version, for Kodi 20 or later.

Install in four steps

  1. In Kodi, open Settings → System → Add-ons and turn on Unknown sources.
  2. Download the repository zip above and copy it where Kodi can open it, or add https://lipelix.github.io/kodi-littlescreen/ as a source in Settings → File manager.
  3. Settings → Add-ons → Install from zip file and pick $repo_zip.
  4. Install from repository → Little Screen repository → Video add-ons → Little Screen. The YouTube add-on, which plays the videos, comes with it. Updates arrive on their own.

What it does

A profile per child

Up to three profiles, each with an age group (4 and under, 5 to 8, 9 to 12) and a look: big pictures without titles, or a list with titles and search.

Their language

The catalogue follows Kodi's language and region. Videos clearly in another language can be hidden; a video whose language is unclear always stays.

Autoplay that stops

The next videos of the same list play after the one picked, up to 50, then it stops. No suggested videos from outside the list.

Private

No account, no sign-in, no analytics. It talks only to youtubekids.com, and what it remembers stays on the device.

How it looks

Good to know

  • Profiles are not a lock. Use Kodi's master lock (Settings → Profiles) to keep children out of settings and other add-ons.
  • Unofficial interface. YouTube can change it at any time; the add-on may then stop working until an update.
  • The catalogue is YouTube's. Little Screen shows what YouTube Kids offers for the age group and region; it does not review videos.

On a Mac, videos do not play?

The YouTube add-on 7.4.x stops with “Could not connect to server” on macOS. In its settings, Advanced → HTTP Server, set the IP address to 0.0.0.0 and restart Kodi. Its server then answers other devices on your local network too (only private addresses), so do it on a network you trust.
